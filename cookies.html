Cookie Policy

Understand how we use cookies and tracking systems on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites.
They are widely used to make web applications work more efficiently and securely.
These files help us remember your configuration, language choices, and course progress.
They contain identifier codes that link your web browser sessions with our databases.
You can manage or completely disable these storage tools using your internet browser.

2. How We Use Cookies

Learnovexa uses cookies to authenticate users and prevent unauthorized access to lessons.
These files record your learning achievements, completed quizzes, and dashboard interface layout.
We also employ analytical cookies to understand how visitors navigate through our pages.
This collective telemetry allows us to constantly optimize page load speeds and structure.
We never utilize cookies to track your behavior across unrelated third-party websites.

3. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

Essential cookies are required for the fundamental operation of our digital academy portal.
Performance cookies collect statistics regarding page errors, link clicks, and traffic volumes.
Functional cookies remember your personalization choices, including screen layout themes and volume levels.
Targeting cookies are utilized by search platforms to show relevant educational courses to you.
All of these cookies function together to deliver a premium and responsive interface.
You can inspect each category individually through your browser developer tools settings pane.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies by default to keep web browsing smooth.
However, you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you.
Please refer to your web browser documentation for step-by-step modification instructions.
Note that disabling essential cookies will immediately interrupt portal login and study tools.
You will need to manually log in each time you access our academy.

5. Cookie Policy Revisions

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our database systems.
All revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated timestamp.
We recommend checking this page regularly to stay informed about our tracking updates.
Your continued use of our platform constitutes acceptance of all revisions published here.
