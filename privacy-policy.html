Privacy Policy

Your privacy is critical to us. Learn about our personal data procedures.

1. Introduction to Our Privacy Policy

At Learnovexa, we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of all our users.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we gather, process, and secure your personal details.
By using our digital platform, you explicitly consent to the terms detailed herein.
We guarantee that we will never sell, lease, or rent your private details to others.
Please review this document thoroughly to understand our professional data management protocols.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly when enrolling in our courses.
This includes your full name, primary email address, telephone number, and payment information.
Additionally, our system collects metadata such as IP addresses, web browser types, and device IDs.
This collection is performed solely to improve your learning experience and portal security.
We only keep this information for as long as is necessary to provide educational services.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we gather is used to deliver high-quality, personalized educational experiences.
We process transactions, manage accounts, and distribute course certificates using your details.
We also send periodic updates about course cohorts, platform upgrades, and research announcements.
You can easily opt-out of promotional communications by clicking the unsubscribe link below.
Our administrative team reviews usage logs to optimize our online class streaming servers.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

Learnovexa does not share your private data with external marketers or database companies.
Information is shared only with verified partners who assist us in operating our platform.
These include payment processors, server hosting firms, and certificate generation systems.
All third-party services are contractually bound to maintain absolute confidentiality at all times.
We may also disclose data if legally required by federal authorities during investigations.
Your security remains our highest priority during all collaborative digital integrations.

5. Security Protocols and Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized database access or alteration.
All transmissions containing payment details use secure sockets layer encryption protocols during checkout.
Our physical servers are maintained in high-security facilities with continuous electronic monitoring systems.
We perform routine technical audits to locate and patch potential software vulnerabilities immediately.
While we strive for absolute security, no method of digital transmission is entirely risk-free.
We recommend utilizing strong passwords and keeping your account login details completely confidential.

6. User Rights and Choices

You possess the right to access, edit, or delete any personal information we store.
To exercise these rights, submit a formal request to our dedicated support email.
We will process your request within thirty business days in accordance with data regulations.
You may also disable web browser cookies through your software preferences pane anytime.
Doing so might limit your access to certain interactive features on our study platform.

7. Contact and Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this privacy statement at any given time.
All modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated modification date.
If you have questions regarding this policy, please reach out to our team.
You can contact us at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
Alternatively, call our official help support helpline at +1-888-777-5845 for direct inquiries.
